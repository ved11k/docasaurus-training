# Getting Started with MyBank Mobile App

Welcome to **MyBank**, a secure mobile banking application that helps you manage your bank accounts, transfer money, pay bills, and access banking services from your mobile device.

This guide explains how to install the app, sign in, and complete the initial setup.   

---

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

- An active MyBank account
- A registered mobile number
- A valid debit card
- An Android or iOS smartphone
- An active internet connection
- Access to your registered mobile number for OTP verification

> **Note:** Your mobile number must be registered with your bank account.

4. Select **Install** or **Get**.
5. Wait for the installation to complete.

## 2. Register Your Account

If you are using the application for the first time:

1. Open the **MyBank** app.
2. Select **Register**.
3. Enter your registered mobile number.
4. Select **Continue**.
5. Enter the One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your mobile number.
6. Enter your account or debit card details.
7. Select **Verify**.

After successful verification, you can create your login credentials.

## 3. Create Your Login Credentials

Create a username and password for your account.

Your password must:

- Contain at least 8 characters
- Include one uppercase letter
- Include one lowercase letter
- Include one number
- Include one special character

For example:

`Bank@1234`

> **Warning:** Never share your password, PIN, or OTP with anyone, including bank employees.

---

## 4. Set Up Your MPIN

An MPIN allows you to quickly access the banking application.

To create your MPIN:

1. Select **Set Up MPIN**.
2. Enter a 6-digit MPIN.
3. Re-enter the MPIN.
4. Select **Confirm**.

Your MPIN is now configured.

---

## 5. Enable Biometric Login

You can use fingerprint or facial recognition to sign in.

1. Go to **Settings > Security**.
2. Select **Biometric Login**.
3. Enable **Fingerprint** or **Face Recognition**.
4. Enter your MPIN to confirm.
5. Complete the biometric verification.

> **Note:** Available biometric options depend on your device.

---

## 6. Sign In

To sign in to your account:

1. Open the **MyBank** app.
2. Enter your username and password.

   **OR**

   Enter your MPIN.

3. Select **Sign In**.

If biometric authentication is enabled, you can also use your fingerprint or face recognition.

## 8. Transfer Money

To transfer money:

1. Select **Transfer Money**.
2. Select the account to transfer money from.
3. Select a beneficiary.
4. Enter the transfer amount.
5. Select **Continue**.
6. Review the transaction details.
   1. check nested level 1
   2. check nested level 1
      1. check nested level 2
7. Select **Confirm**.
8. Enter the OTP or MPIN when prompted.

A confirmation message appears after the transaction is completed.

## 9. Troubleshooting

### I did not receive the OTP

Try the following:

- Check your mobile network connection.
  - nested level 1
    - nested level 2
      - nested level 3
        - nested level 4
          - nested level 5
- Verify your registered mobile number.
- Wait for a few minutes.
- Select **Resend OTP**.

### I forgot my password

1. On the login screen, select **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter your registered mobile number.
3. Complete OTP verification.
4. Create a new password.
5. Sign in using your new password.

### The app is not opening

Try the following:

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Close and reopen the application.
3. Update the application.
4. Restart your mobile device.

If the problem continues, contact customer support.

## Security Best Practices

Follow these recommendations to keep your account secure:

- Never share your OTP, MPIN, or password.
- Do not save banking passwords in shared devices.
- Avoid banking over public Wi-Fi.
- Always sign out after completing your banking activities.
- Keep your banking application updated.
- Report suspicious transactions immediately.
