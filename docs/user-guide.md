## Getting Started

### Sign In

1. Open the banking app.
2. Enter your username and password.
3. Select **Sign In**.
4. Complete verification if prompted.

> **Tip:** Never share your password, PIN, or OTP with anyone.

## View Account Balance

1. Sign in to the app.
2. Go to **Accounts**.
3. Select an account.
4. View the available balance and recent transactions.

## Transfer Money

1. Go to **Transfers**.
2. Select the source account.
3. Choose or add a beneficiary.
4. Enter the transfer amount.
5. Review the transaction details.
6. Select **Confirm**.
7. Complete authentication.

## Open a Fixed Deposit

1. Go to **Deposits**.
2. Select **Open Fixed Deposit**.
3. Choose the source account.
4. Enter the deposit amount and tenure.
5. Review the interest rate and maturity details.
6. Confirm the request.

## Troubleshooting

### Transaction Failed

If a transaction fails:

- Check your internet connection.
- Verify the beneficiary details.
- Confirm that sufficient funds are available.
- Check whether transaction limits have been reached.
- Try again later if the banking service is temporarily unavailable.

### Cannot Sign In

- Check your username and password.
- Make sure Caps Lock is turned off.
- Use **Forgot Password** if necessary.
- Contact customer support if your account is locked.

## Security Best Practices

- Use a strong, unique password.
- Enable biometric authentication when available.
- Never disclose your OTP or PIN.
- Sign out when using a shared device.
- Keep the banking app updated.

## Support

For additional assistance, use the **Help** or **Contact Us** option in the banking app.
