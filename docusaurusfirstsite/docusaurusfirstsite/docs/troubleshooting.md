# Banking App Troubleshooting Guide

## Overview

Use this guide to resolve common issues when using the banking app.

## Cannot Sign In

**Possible causes:**

- Incorrect username or password
- Unstable internet connection
- Account temporarily locked
- App version is outdated

**Solution:**

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Verify your username and password.
3. Make sure Caps Lock is turned off.
4. Select **Forgot Password** if you cannot remember your password.
5. Update the banking app if an update is available.
6. Contact customer support if your account remains locked.

> **Security note:** Never share your password, PIN, or OTP with anyone.

## Fund Transfer Failed

**Possible causes:**

- Insufficient account balance
- Incorrect beneficiary details
- Transaction limit exceeded
- Banking service temporarily unavailable

**Solution:**

1. Check your available balance.
2. Verify the beneficiary details.
3. Check your daily transaction limit.
4. Confirm that you have a stable internet connection.
5. Check the transaction history before trying again.
6. Retry the transfer later if the service is unavailable.

> **Important:** Do not immediately repeat a transfer if the amount was deducted. Check the transaction status first.

## OTP Not Received

1. Check that your registered mobile number is active.
2. Verify that your phone has network coverage.
3. Wait a few minutes for the OTP.
4. Select **Resend OTP** if available.
5. Contact customer support if the issue continues.

## App Is Slow or Not Responding

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Close and reopen the app.
3. Restart your device.
4. Update the app to the latest version.
5. Clear the app cache if supported by your device.
6. Contact support if the problem continues.

## Unable to Add a Beneficiary

- Verify the account number.
- Check the bank or branch details.
- Complete all mandatory fields.
- Complete OTP or other required authentication.
- Wait for the beneficiary activation period, if applicable.

## Transaction Shows as Pending

1. Open **Transaction History**.
2. Check the current transaction status.
3. Wait for the bank to complete processing.
4. Do not repeat the transaction while it is pending.
5. Contact customer support if the status does not change within the expected processing time.

## Contact Support

If you cannot resolve an issue:

1. Open **Help** or **Contact Us** in the banking app.
2. Note the transaction reference number, if applicable.
3. Provide the error message and relevant transaction details.
4. Do not share your password, PIN, CVV, or OTP.
